# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Source of truth: the HTML mocks in `docs/mocks/` (34 screens, 155 mocks) and the cast and
assumptions in `docs/mocks/README.md`. Every requirement below traces to something visible in a
mock. Words follow the domain vocabulary in `AGENTS.md`: Team, Member, Day (`Tentative`,
`Requested`, `Confirmed`), Holiday, Manager.

## L1-001: Access and account
People sign in with an email and password or with their Google account, recover a forgotten
password, join a team by accepting an invitation (with a password or with Google), keep their
own profile and password up to date, and sign out. Greenlit is invite-only for members: there is
no self sign-up for members, and the only way to start without an invitation is to continue with
Google, which creates a manager account that goes on to set up a team.

## L1-002: Team setup and membership
A manager sets up a team with a name, a time zone, a public-holiday calendar and its members,
then maintains the team's settings and membership: inviting, changing roles and yearly days, and
removing members while keeping their history.

## L1-003: Team calendar
Everyone on a team sees the same month as a sheet: members down the side, weekday initials and
day numbers across the top, every cell coloured and labelled by its state, with each member's
days left and a daily count of who is out.

## L1-004: Marking days
On the calendar, a member sets their own days to tentative, requested or clear, and a manager
also confirms days, by clicking, dragging or using the keyboard, with every change undoable.

## L1-005: Time-off requests
A member asks for a run of days with an optional note, follows and cancels their own requests,
and a manager approves or declines each request, alone or in bulk, with the overlaps in view.

## L1-006: Allowances
Each member has a yearly allowance of days, adjusted by the manager with a reason. Greenlit shows
how many days each member has used, booked, requested and left, and flags when requests would
take someone over their allowance.

## L1-007: Year overview
Everyone sees the whole team's year in one view, month by month, together with each person's
allowance usage, and can open any month from it.

## L1-008: Person page
Each member has a page with their allowance, a year at a glance and their upcoming and past time
off; on it a manager adjusts the allowance, edits working days and removes the member.

## L1-009: Holidays and working days
Weekends, team holidays and the days a part-time member does not work are red, are never
counted against allowance and cannot be booked. The manager maintains the team's holidays and
working days.

## L1-010: Coverage
The manager sets the fewest people who should be in on any working day, and Greenlit flags days
that fall below it on the calendar, in request decisions and on approval.

## L1-011: Email notifications
Greenlit emails the manager about new requests and emails members about decisions, as switched
on in the team's settings. It never sends daily digests. Every email Greenlit sends, including
invitations and password resets, is a branded HTML email from Greenlit's own address, delivered
for real in production, with a plain-text alternative.

## L1-012: Feedback, errors and connectivity
Every action reports its outcome in place or in a toast, every failure says what happened and
that nothing was lost, and Greenlit keeps working read-only while offline, with clear pages for
missing, forbidden, failed and expired states.

## L1-013: Accessible sheet
Every interaction on the sheet works with a pointer, touch and the keyboard, and a cell's colour
is never the only carrier of its state.

## L1-014: Marketing page
A public single page, served separately from the app, explains Greenlit to a manager who still
keeps the vacation spreadsheet and sends them to create a team or sign in. It shows the sheet,
a short narrated video and the three colours, and never needs a session.
