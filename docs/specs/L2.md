# L2 - Detailed Requirements

Every criterion below is drawn from `docs/mocks/`; quoted text is the mocks' exact copy. The
examples use the mocks' cast: Design team (Europe/London, United Kingdom holidays), manager
Maya Chen, members Ana Ruiz, Ben Okafor, Cleo Park, Dev Patel (works Mon–Thu), Elif Yılmaz and
Finn Murphy, with "today" Friday 6 November 2026. Names, dates and numbers in the examples come
from that data and stand for any team.

---

## L2-001: Sign in
**Traces to:** L1-001

A person signs in with email and password. Greenlit is invite-only, and the sign-in page says
so. Mocks: `pages/sign-in/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-out visitor, when they open the sign-in page, then it must show "Sign in", "Welcome back. Your team's time off is waiting.", the fields Email and Password, the button "Sign in", the link "Forgot your password?" and, below the "or" divider and "Continue with Google" of L2-051, the note "New here? Continue with Google to set up a team, or ask your manager to send you an invitation."
2. Given both fields are empty, when the visitor presses "Sign in", then the page must show "Fix 2 things to continue" with "Enter your email address." on Email and "Enter your password." on Password, and must not submit.
3. Given an email and password that do not match an account, when the visitor signs in, then the page must show "Email or password doesn't match" and "Check them and try again. If you've forgotten your password, you can reset it."
4. Given valid credentials, when the visitor signs in, then the button must show a busy state while submitting, and then "Signed in", "Welcome back, Maya", "Taking you to your calendar…" and a "Go to the calendar now" link, followed by the calendar.

## L2-002: Forgot password
**Traces to:** L1-001

A person asks for a password reset link by email. Mocks: `pages/forgot-password/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the forgot-password page, when it opens, then it must show "Reset your password", "Enter your email and we'll send you a link.", the Email field with "We'll send a link to reset your password.", the button "Send reset link" and the link "Back to sign in".
2. Given an email is entered, when the person presses "Send reset link", then the page must show "Check your email" and "If maya@northwind.studio has an account, a reset link is on its way. It works for one hour.", without revealing whether the account exists.
3. Given the email cannot be sent, when the person presses "Send reset link", then the page must show "We couldn't send the email" and "Something went wrong on our side. Try again in a moment."
4. Given the Email field is empty or not an email address, when the person submits, then the field must show an error and the form must not submit.

## L2-003: Reset password
**Traces to:** L1-001

A reset link lets a person choose a new password once, within one hour. Mocks:
`pages/reset-password/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a valid reset link, when the person opens it, then the page must show "Choose a new password", "Pick something you haven't used here before.", New password with "Use at least 10 characters.", Confirm new password and the button "Update password".
2. Given a new password shorter than 10 characters, when the person submits, then the page must show "New password must be at least 10 characters" and must not change the password.
3. Given the confirmation differs from the new password, when the person submits, then the page must show "Passwords don't match" in the summary and "This doesn't match the new password." on the confirmation field.
4. Given a reset link older than one hour or already used, when the person opens it or submits, then the page must show "This link has expired", "Reset links work for one hour and only once. Request a new one to continue." and a "Request a new link" action.
5. Given a valid link and matching passwords of at least 10 characters, when the person submits, then the password must change and the page must show "Password updated", "You can sign in with your new password." and a "Sign in" action.
6. Given the password cannot be updated for a reason other than the link, when the person submits, then the page must show "We couldn’t update your password" and "Something went wrong on our side. Nothing was changed. Try again in a moment.", keep the fields filled and leave the link usable. Mock: `pages/reset-password/failed.html`.

## L2-004: Accept an invitation
**Traces to:** L1-001

An invited person joins the team by setting their name and password. Invitations work for seven
days. Mocks: `pages/accept-invite/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a valid invitation from Maya Chen to Design team, when the invitee opens the link, then the page must show "Join Design team", "Maya Chen invited you to share the team's time off.", Your name, Email with "This is where your invitation was sent.", Choose a password, Confirm password and the button "Join the team".
2. Given the invitation was sent to an address, when the page opens, then Email must hold that address.
3. Given a password shorter than 10 characters or a confirmation that does not match, when the invitee submits, then the page must show the matching errors from L2-003 AC2 and AC3 and must not create the account.
4. Given an invitation older than seven days, when the invitee opens it, then the page must show "This invitation has expired", "Invitations work for seven days. Ask Maya Chen to send you a new one." and a "Go to sign in" action.
5. Given valid input, when the invitee presses "Join the team", then the account must be created, the invitee must be signed in as a member of the team, and the member's row must keep the days already on it.
6. Given a link that was already used, was replaced by a newer invitation, or is not recognised, when the invitee opens it or submits, then the page must show the same expired state as AC4 (a link that is not recognised names no one: "Ask whoever invited you to send you a new one.") and must not create an account.
7. Given the invitation cannot be loaded, when the page opens, then it must show "We couldn't load your invitation", "Something went wrong on our side. Nothing was changed. Try again in a moment." and a "Try again" action; given the account cannot be created, when the invitee submits, then the page must show "We couldn't create your account" with the same sentence, keep the fields and create nothing.
8. Given an empty name, when the invitee submits, then the page must show "Enter your name." under Your name and must not create the account; given the invitee's email already has a Greenlit account, when they open the invitation, then L2-058 applies instead of this form: no second account is created and the invitation adds a membership to the existing account.
9. Given the account was created, when the invitee lands on the calendar, then their row must be marked as theirs ("you") and carry the role the manager chose in the invitation.

## L2-005: Account page
**Traces to:** L1-001

A signed-in person edits their display name and email and changes their password. Mocks:
`pages/account/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-in person, when they open Account, then it must show Profile ("How you appear to your team.") with Display name, Email ("This is how you sign in.") and "Save changes"; Password ("Use at least 10 characters.") with Current password, New password, Confirm new password and "Update password"; and Sign out ("You'll need your email and password to get back in.").
2. Given a new password shorter than 10 characters and a confirmation that does not match, when the person presses "Update password", then the page must show "Fix 2 things to continue", "New password must be at least 10 characters" and "Passwords don't match", with "This doesn’t match the new password." on the confirmation field.
3. Given the correct current password and a valid new password, when the person presses "Update password", then the password must change and the page must show "Password updated".
4. Given the account cannot be loaded, when the page opens, then it must show "We couldn't load your account", "Something went wrong on our side. Nothing was changed." and "Try again".
5. Given a signed-in person who changes their display name, when they press "Save changes", then the name must be saved, show "Saved" beside the button, and appear in the account menu, on the sheet row and everywhere the team sees them.
6. Given an email already used by another account, when the person presses "Save changes", then the page must show "That email is already in use." on the Email field and change nothing; when the email is free, then it is saved and is the one they sign in with next.
7. Given a wrong current password, when the person presses "Update password", then the page must show "That isn’t your current password." on the Current password field and change nothing; after a successful change the new password signs in and the old one no longer does.

## L2-008: Create a team
**Traces to:** L1-002

A manager sets up a team and its rows in one form. Mocks: `pages/create-team/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the create-team page, when it opens, then it must show "Set up your team", "Name it, pick a time zone and add the people who'll share the sheet.", Team name, Time zone with "Weekends and holidays are worked out in this time zone.", Public holidays with "You can add or remove holidays at any time.", and "Who is on the team?" with "Add a name for every row on your sheet. Add an email to send an invitation."
2. Given the Time zone field, when it opens, then it must offer London (GMT+0 / BST), Dublin, Paris, Berlin (CET), Rome (CET), New York (ET) and Toronto (ET); given the Public holidays field, then it must offer United Kingdom, Ireland, United States, Canada, Italy and "None, I'll add my own".
3. Given the member list, when the manager presses "Add another member", then a new row with "Member N name" and "Member N email (optional)" must appear.
4. Given an empty team name, when the manager presses "Create team", then the page must show "Give your team a name" on the field and must not create the team. Members are optional: given no member rows filled in, or a member row with no name (with or without an email), when the manager presses "Create team", then the page must show no error for that row and the row must be left out of the team.
5. Given valid input, when the manager presses "Create team", then the team must be created with the chosen time zone and that country's public holidays as team holidays, every named member (none is required) must become a row, every named member with an email must be sent an invitation (L2-010), and the creator must be the team's manager.
6. Given the team cannot be created, when the manager submits, then the page must show "We couldn't create your team" and "Something went wrong on our side. Nothing was saved, so you can try again."
7. Given the team is created, when the page updates, then it must show "You're all set", "Your sheet is ready.", "{team name} is ready" with "We've emailed invitations to N people. Anyone without an email is on the calendar now, and you can invite them later from Settings." (or, when no one was invited, "Everyone is on the calendar now, and you can invite them later from Settings.") and "Open the calendar", which opens the new team's calendar.
8. Given a signed-in person who is not on a team, when they open any app screen, then Greenlit must open the create-team page. Given a person who is already on a team, as its manager or only as a member (L2-055), when they open the create-team page, then it must open as "Set up another team" (L2-054 AC5) and creating the team must succeed, with them as the new team's manager; any signed-in person may create a team, and no request to create one is refused because the person is already on a team.

## L2-009: Team settings
**Traces to:** L1-002

Settings has the sections Team, Allowances, Work week & holidays, Coverage, Notifications and
School calendar (L2-059), each saved on its own. Only managers can change them. Mocks:
`pages/settings/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a manager, when they open Settings, then the Team section must show "Everyone who appears on the calendar. Managers can approve requests and edit any row.", Team name, Time zone (the options of L2-008 AC2) and one row per member with avatar, name, email, a Role control (Member / Manager) labelled "Role for {name}" and a yearly-days field labelled "Yearly days for {name}" with "days / yr", plus "Invite a member" and "Save changes".
2. Given a manager changes the team name, time zone, a role or a member's yearly days, when they press that section's "Save changes", then the change must be saved for everyone and the button must show "Saving…" and then "Saved".
3. Given a member, when they open Settings, then every section must be read-only and the page must show "Only managers can change team settings. Ask Maya Chen if something needs updating."
4. Given a section with no edits, when the manager presses "Save changes", then Greenlit must show the toast "No changes to save" with "Everything already matches what you entered."
5. Given the save fails, when the manager presses "Save changes", then Greenlit must show "We couldn't save your settings" (or the toast "We couldn't save your changes") with "Nothing was changed. Check your connection and try again." and "Try again", and the values must stay as entered.
6. Given the settings cannot be loaded, when the page opens, then it must show "We couldn't load your settings", "Something went wrong on our side. Nothing was changed." and "Try again".
7. Given the team settings are saved, when the save succeeds, then Greenlit must show the banner "Team settings saved" with "Everyone sees the new settings straight away."
8. Given a manager, when they open Settings, then the School calendar section of L2-059 must follow Notifications, off by default, and turning it on or off must be saved with that section's "Save changes" like any other section; given a member, then it must be read-only as in AC3.

## L2-013: Day cells

Each cell shows one member's day. Mocks: every calendar mock; `docs/mocks/README.md` (glyphs).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

## L2-022: Requests ledger for the manager

`approved`, `declined`, `empty`, `long`, `error`.

## L2-062: Import a school calendar
**Traces to:** L1-016

The manager can upload a school's calendar file (`.ics`) for one chosen child, which adds that
school's closures to the child's row. It is a one-time upload: Greenlit does not fetch or refresh
a calendar by itself. A file may be up to 1 MB and hold up to 500 events; every all-day event in
it becomes a closure, and timed or repeating events are left out (ADR-015). Mocks:
`dialogs/import-calendar/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the school calendar is on, when the manager presses "Import a calendar", then a dialog must show "Import a school calendar", "Add a school's closures from its calendar file (.ics) to one child's row.", "Child", "Calendar file" with "Choose an .ics file" and "Up to 1 MB. All-day events become closures.", "Cancel" and "Import"; when they choose a child and a file and press "Import", then the button must show "Importing…", each all-day event in it must become a closure of that child, with the event's title as the label and its dates as the range, and the dialog must show "Imported 9 closures for Leo" with "They're on Leo's row now." and "Done".
2. Given a child who already has closures from an earlier import, when the manager chooses that child, then the dialog must say "Importing again replaces the 9 closures from Leo's last import. Closures you added yourself stay."; when a file is uploaded for the same child, then the closures from the earlier import must be replaced by those in the new file.
3. Given a child with closures the manager typed in (L2-061), when a file is imported for that child, then the typed closures must be unchanged; an imported closure the manager has edited counts as typed from then on.
4. Given two children at different schools, when the manager imports a file for one, then the other child's row must be unchanged.
5. Given a file that is not a readable calendar, is larger than 1 MB, holds more than 500 events or holds no all-day event, when the manager uploads it, then the dialog must show "That file isn't a calendar we can read" with "Choose an .ics file of up to 1 MB exported from the school's calendar. Nothing was changed.", the API must answer 400 with the code `calendar_unreadable`, and nothing must change; given the upload fails for any other reason, then it must show "We couldn't import the calendar" with "Check your connection and try again. Nothing was changed."
6. Given an imported closure, when the manager edits or deletes it, then it must behave as any closure (L2-061).
7. Given a signed-in member who is not the manager, when they look for an import control, then none must be offered, and the API must refuse an upload with 403.
