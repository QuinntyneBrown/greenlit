# L2 - Detailed Requirements

Every criterion below is drawn from `docs/mocks/`; quoted text is the mocks' exact copy. The
examples use the mocks' cast: Design team (Europe/London, United Kingdom holidays), manager
Maya Chen, members Ana Ruiz, Ben Okafor, Cleo Park, Dev Patel (works Mon–Thu), Elif Yılmaz and
Finn Murphy, with "today" Friday 6 November 2026. Names, dates and numbers in the examples come
from that data and stand for any team.

---

## L2-001: Sign in
**Traces to:** L1-001

A person signs in with email and password. Greenlit is invite-only, and the sign-in page says
so. Mocks: `pages/sign-in/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a signed-out visitor, when they open the sign-in page, then it must show "Sign in", "Welcome back. Your team's time off is waiting.", the fields Email and Password, the button "Sign in", the link "Forgot your password?" and the note "New here? Greenlit is invite-only. Ask your manager to send you an invitation."
2. Given both fields are empty, when the visitor presses "Sign in", then the page must show "Fix 2 things to continue" with "Enter your email address." on Email and "Enter your password." on Password, and must not submit.
3. Given an email and password that do not match an account, when the visitor signs in, then the page must show "Email or password doesn't match" and "Check them and try again. If you've forgotten your password, you can reset it."
4. Given valid credentials, when the visitor signs in, then the button must show a busy state while submitting, and then "Signed in", "Welcome back, Maya", "Taking you to your calendar…" and a "Go to the calendar now" link, followed by the calendar.

## L2-002: Forgot password
**Traces to:** L1-001

A person asks for a password reset link by email. Mocks: `pages/forgot-password/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the forgot-password page, when it opens, then it must show "Reset your password", "Enter your email and we'll send you a link.", the Email field with "We'll send a link to reset your password.", the button "Send reset link" and the link "Back to sign in".
2. Given an email is entered, when the person presses "Send reset link", then the page must show "Check your email" and "If maya@northwind.studio has an account, a reset link is on its way. It works for one hour.", without revealing whether the account exists.
3. Given the email cannot be sent, when the person presses "Send reset link", then the page must show "We couldn't send the email" and "Something went wrong on our side. Try again in a moment."
4. Given the Email field is empty or not an email address, when the person submits, then the field must show an error and the form must not submit.

## L2-003: Reset password
**Traces to:** L1-001

A reset link lets a person choose a new password once, within one hour. Mocks:
`pages/reset-password/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a valid reset link, when the person opens it, then the page must show "Choose a new password", "Pick something you haven't used here before.", New password with "Use at least 10 characters.", Confirm new password and the button "Update password".
2. Given a new password shorter than 10 characters, when the person submits, then the page must show "New password must be at least 10 characters" and must not change the password.
3. Given the confirmation differs from the new password, when the person submits, then the page must show "Passwords don't match" in the summary and "This doesn't match the new password." on the confirmation field.
4. Given a reset link older than one hour or already used, when the person opens it or submits, then the page must show "This link has expired", "Reset links work for one hour and only once. Request a new one to continue." and a "Request a new link" action.
5. Given a valid link and matching passwords of at least 10 characters, when the person submits, then the password must change and the page must show "Password updated", "You can sign in with your new password." and a "Sign in" action.

## L2-004: Accept an invitation
**Traces to:** L1-001

An invited person joins the team by setting their name and password. Invitations work for seven
days. Mocks: `pages/accept-invite/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a valid invitation from Maya Chen to Design team, when the invitee opens the link, then the page must show "Join Design team", "Maya Chen invited you to share the team's time off.", Your name, Email with "This is where your invitation was sent.", Choose a password, Confirm password and the button "Join the team".
2. Given the invitation was sent to an address, when the page opens, then Email must hold that address.
3. Given a password shorter than 10 characters or a confirmation that does not match, when the invitee submits, then the page must show the matching errors from L2-003 AC2 and AC3 and must not create the account.
4. Given an invitation older than seven days, when the invitee opens it, then the page must show "This invitation has expired", "Invitations work for seven days. Ask Maya Chen to send you a new one." and a "Go to sign in" action.
5. Given valid input, when the invitee presses "Join the team", then the account must be created, the invitee must be signed in as a member of the team, and the member's row must keep the days already on it.

## L2-008: Create a team
**Traces to:** L1-002

A manager sets up a team and its rows in one form. Mocks: `pages/create-team/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given the create-team page, when it opens, then it must show "Set up your team", "Name it, pick a time zone and add the people who'll share the sheet.", Team name, Time zone with "Weekends and holidays are worked out in this time zone.", Public holidays with "You can add or remove holidays at any time.", and "Who is on the team?" with "Add a name for every row on your sheet. Add an email to send an invitation."
2. Given the Time zone field, when it opens, then it must offer London (GMT+0 / BST), Dublin, Paris, Berlin (CET) and New York (ET); given the Public holidays field, then it must offer United Kingdom, Ireland, United States and "None, I'll add my own".
3. Given the member list, when the manager presses "Add another member", then a new row with "Member N name" and "Member N email (optional)" must appear.
4. Given an empty team name or a member row with an email but no name, when the manager presses "Create team", then the page must show "Give your team a name" and "Give this member a name" on the fields at fault and must not create the team.
5. Given valid input, when the manager presses "Create team", then the team must be created with the chosen time zone and that country's public holidays as team holidays, every named member must become a row, every member with an email must be sent an invitation (L2-010), and the creator must be the team's manager.
6. Given the team cannot be created, when the manager submits, then the page must show "We couldn't create your team" and "Something went wrong on our side. Nothing was saved, so you can try again."
7. Given the team is created, when the page updates, then it must show "You're all set", "Your sheet is ready.", "{team name} is ready" with "We've emailed invitations to N people. Anyone without an email is on the calendar now, and you can invite them later from Settings." (or, when no one was invited, "Everyone is on the calendar now, and you can invite them later from Settings.") and "Open the calendar", which opens the new team's calendar.
8. Given a signed-in person who is not on a team, when they open any app screen, then Greenlit must open the create-team page; given a person who is already on a team, when they open the create-team page, then Greenlit must open the calendar, and a request to create a second team must be refused with "You're already on a team."

## L2-009: Team settings
**Traces to:** L1-002

Settings has the sections Team, Allowances, Work week & holidays, Coverage and Notifications,
each saved on its own. Only managers can change them. Mocks: `pages/settings/*`.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a manager, when they open Settings, then the Team section must show "Everyone who appears on the calendar. Managers can approve requests and edit any row.", Team name, Time zone (the options of L2-008 AC2) and one row per member with avatar, name, email, a Role control (Member / Manager) labelled "Role for {name}" and a yearly-days field labelled "Yearly days for {name}" with "days / yr", plus "Invite a member" and "Save changes".
2. Given a manager changes the team name, time zone, a role or a member's yearly days, when they press that section's "Save changes", then the change must be saved for everyone and the button must show "Saving…" and then "Saved".
3. Given a member, when they open Settings, then every section must be read-only and the page must show "Only managers can change team settings. Ask Maya Chen if something needs updating."
4. Given a section with no edits, when the manager presses "Save changes", then Greenlit must show the toast "No changes to save" with "Everything already matches what you entered."
5. Given the save fails, when the manager presses "Save changes", then Greenlit must show "We couldn't save your settings" (or the toast "We couldn't save your changes") with "Nothing was changed. Check your connection and try again." and "Try again", and the values must stay as entered.
6. Given the settings cannot be loaded, when the page opens, then it must show "We couldn't load your settings", "Something went wrong on our side. Nothing was changed." and "Try again".
7. Given the team settings are saved, when the save succeeds, then Greenlit must show the banner "Team settings saved" with "Everyone sees the new settings straight away."

## L2-013: Day cells
**Traces to:** L1-003

Each cell shows one member's day. Mocks: every calendar mock; `docs/mocks/README.md` (glyphs).

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a working day with no day stored, when the cell renders, then it must be empty and its accessible name must be "{member}, {Day d Mon}: Working".
2. Given a weekend day, or a day the member does not work (L2-039, L2-040), when the cell renders, then it must be red and its accessible name must end "Not working".
3. Given a team holiday, when the cell renders, then it must be red for every member and its accessible name must end with the holiday's name, for example "Christmas Day".
4. Given a tentative, requested or confirmed day, when the cell renders, then it must be blue, yellow or green respectively, show the glyph "?", "…" or "✓", and its accessible name must end "Tentative", "Requested" or "Confirmed".
5. Given a request is pending, when the calendar renders, then its days must show as requested; given it is approved, then they must show as confirmed.
