## Roadmap

1. sheet: the last gaps of view-month and mark-days (select days and the keyboard are built)
2. identity-and-access and teams: the account journeys (sign out, manage account) and notification settings (invite, accept, reset password, holidays, working days, the coverage minimum and team settings are built)
3. requests: the member's request detail (send, approve, review, decline, my requests and cancel are built; days left is built with allowances; the Decisions switch arrives with notifications)
4. web-experience: connectivity and the remaining error pages (toasts are built)
5. allowances: year, person page and adjustments (figures and the Settings Allowances section are built under ADR-007)
