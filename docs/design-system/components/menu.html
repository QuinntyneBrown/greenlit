Menu

A menu is a short, anchored list of actions or destinations that opens from a button. In Greenlit there is one: the account menu behind the avatar in the top bar.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

Use a menu to tuck a handful of secondary commands or destinations behind one trigger when there is no room (or no reason) to show them all: the account menu holds My time off, Account and Sign out. Items either navigate (<a>) or act (<button>).

A menu is not a form control (use a select to choose a value), not site navigation (use app navigation or tabs) and not a place for the primary action of a screen (use a button). Items that need a confirmation open a dialog; "Sign out" does exactly that.

The panel is the .menu list; .menu-anchor is a relative wrapper for any trigger, and .user-menu is the account-menu wrapper that right-aligns the panel to the avatar.

Anatomy

  1. Identity header.user-menu__who: the signed-in person's name and role. Not interactive, not a menu item (role="none" wrapper).
  2. Divider.menu__divider groups navigation apart from account actions.
  3. Item.menu__item with role="menuitem": an optional 1rem icon, then the label. 32px high, --radius-sm.
  4. Panel.menu: raised surface, 1px border, --shadow-3, role="menu" with an accessible name.

The trigger

Any button can be the trigger: aria-haspopup="menu" plus aria-expanded. The avatar trigger is a .avatar-btn.

Sizes

Not applicable: One size: items are 32px high (<code>--control-height-sm</code>) and the panel is at least 12rem wide (15rem for the account menu). There is no size prop.

States

Items have default, hover, focus (a focused item looks like a hovered one, driven by roving focus) and disabled. The trigger shows its open state through aria-expanded, which tints the avatar button.

  • A menu with no items is not shown: hide the trigger instead.
  • The panel enters with a short scale-in (scale-in, --duration-fast) from its anchor corner; leaving is instant.

Responsive behaviour

  • The account menu opens from the right edge (right: 0, origin top right) so it never leaves the viewport; its minimum width is 15rem, which fits a 360px screen with margin.
  • A generic .menu opens from the left edge of its .menu-anchor. Near the right edge of the viewport position it from the right instead.
  • Menus do not turn into bottom sheets on phones; at 360px the panel is simply anchored under the trigger. Items stay 32px high with full-width hit areas.
  • Menus must not be taller than the viewport; with more than about eight items use a different pattern (a page or a dialog).

Theming

The panel is --color-bg-surface-raised with --color-border-default and --shadow-3; items read --color-fg-default, hover/focus --color-bg-subtle, disabled --color-fg-disabled and danger --color-danger-fg. In dark mode the raised surface is lighter than the page, so the panel separates by surface level as well as by shadow.

/* A denser menu for a table's row actions */
.row-menu .menu__item { min-height: var(--control-height-sm); font: var(--text-caption); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA APG Menu Button: a button with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded controlling a role="menu" of role="menuitem"s. Wrapper <li role="none"> elements keep list semantics out of the way. Use menus for commands; a set of links that should be browsed is a list of links.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter, Space, ↓ (on trigger)Opens the menu and focuses the first item. ↑ opens and focuses the last.
↓ / ↑Moves to the next / previous item, wrapping at the ends.
Home / EndMoves to the first / last item.
Type a characterMoves to the next item whose label starts with that character.
Enter / Space (on item)Activates the item (follows the link or runs the action) and closes the menu.
EscCloses the menu and returns focus to the trigger.
TabCloses the menu and moves on in page order; the menu does not trap focus.

Focus

Focus moves into the menu on open (first item) and returns to the trigger on close. The focused item uses the hover fill with no outline, so that fill must stay distinguishable from the panel in both themes; the trigger keeps the shared focus ring.

Labelling

The trigger's name says what the menu is for ("Account menu for Maya Chen"); the panel repeats a short name in aria-label="Account". Icons are aria-hidden; the label is the name. The identity header is outside the menu items so it is not announced as a command. A disabled item uses aria-disabled="true" so it remains discoverable.

Motion

A 120ms scale-in from the anchor corner. It is purely cosmetic; under prefers-reduced-motion the duration token collapses and the panel appears at once.

Touch

Items are 32px high inside a 12rem+ wide panel with full-width hit areas (well above the 24px minimum, below the 44px comfortable size). The trigger is the avatar button; give frequent actions their own button rather than a menu item.

Content

  • Item labels are verbs or destinations in sentence case: "My time off", "Account", "Sign out". No ellipsis, no trailing punctuation.
  • Order by frequency, then put destructive or session-ending items last, separated by a divider.
  • An item that opens a confirmation dialog does not need "…"; the dialog does the asking.
  • The identity header shows who and what role ("Manager · maya@northwind.studio"), never a greeting.
