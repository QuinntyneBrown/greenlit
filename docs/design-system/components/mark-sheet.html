Component · Sheet and brand

Mark sheet

The panel fixed to the foot of a phone while days are marked: it names the member and the days picked, asks for the last day, then offers Request, Tentative, Confirm and Clear. It is not a dialog.

Overview

The mark sheet (.mark-sheet in a .mark-sheet-region, Angular gl-mark-sheet) is the phone's counterpart of the selection bar. Marking time off on a phone is two taps on one member's row of the week strip, a first and a last day; the sheet guides that and then applies a status to the range. It slides up from the foot of the screen in two states: picking ("From Mon 23 Nov", "Just this day") after the first tap, and range (the dates, the working days, any overage and clash, and the actions) after the second.

It is a non-modal <section> labelled "Selected days", not a dialog: no backdrop, no focus trap, and the week tabs and cells behind it stay usable, because the person often changes week before the second tap. Use it only for marking days on the phone calendar; from 48rem up the selection bar does this job. When nothing is selected, the foot shows the hint (.phone-hint) instead.

Anatomy

  1. Member.mark-sheet__who: the member's full name, caption, muted.
  2. Range.mark-sheet__range: an h2 in the display face (--text-h4): "From Mon 23 Nov" while picking, "Mon 23 – Thu 26 Nov" for a range.
  3. Meta.mark-sheet__meta: the instruction while picking, then the working-day count, with .mark-sheet__warn for an overage.
  4. Cancel.mark-sheet__cancel: a small ghost icon button named "Cancel"; the same as Esc.
  5. Clash.mark-sheet__clash: who else is out on the range's working days, on the subtle surface. Only when someone is.
  6. Primary action.btn--primary "Request these days", full width, with a requested swatch.
  7. Status actions"Tentative" and, for the manager, "Confirm": secondary buttons sharing a row, each with its swatch.
  8. Clear.btn--link "Clear these days", full width, last.

Sizes

Not applicable: the sheet has one size: it spans the viewport's width, its content is held to the shell's width, and its height follows its content.

Nothing selected, member: "your row" instead of "someone’s row". A member keeps "Request time off" in the title row.
  • Cleared: Cancel or Esc clears the selection and shows the toast "Selection cleared" with "Pick days to start again.". Opening a date or a short-days run clears it without a toast.
  • Starting again: a tap on another row's cell restarts picking from that cell; the sheet stays and its member changes.
  • Offline: no cell takes a tap, so the sheet cannot open; the hint still shows.
  • Disabled: not used. An action that does not apply is removed.
  • Print: the sheet and the hint are part of the phone layout only; they are not designed for print.

The sheet is on --color-bg-surface with --shadow-4, so in the dark theme it reads as the most raised surface above the page.

Responsive behaviour

  • The sheet and the hint exist only below 48rem, with the week strip. From 48rem up the selection bar serves the month grid.
  • The region is position: fixed; inset-inline: 0; bottom: 0: full width with its top corners rounded (--radius-xl), the content held in .shell so it aligns with the page.
  • Actions are a two-column grid: the primary action and Clear span both columns, Tentative and Confirm share a row; with data-single the single button spans both. At 320px "Request these days" still fits on one line.
  • The page keeps 9rem of bottom padding (.phone-week) under the hint and 20rem while the sheet is up (.phone-week[data-sheet]), so the sheet never covers the last member row.
  • Bottom padding is --space-6, which keeps the actions clear of a phone's home indicator.

Theming

The sheet reads --color-bg-surface, --color-border-default, --shadow-4, --radius-xl and --z-dropdown; text reads --color-fg-default and --color-fg-muted, the overage --color-warning-fg, and the clash line --color-bg-subtle. The hint uses the same surface and border on --z-sticky, below the sheet. Buttons are the standard buttons.

/* A flatter sheet for a product without elevation in dark mode */
[data-theme="dark"] .mark-sheet { box-shadow: none; border-top-color: var(--color-border-strong); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-label="Selected days">: a named region landmark, deliberately not role="dialog". Like the selection bar it is non-modal: no backdrop, no aria-modal, no focus trap and no inert page, because the person must be able to change week and tap the last day while it is open. The range is an h2, so the sheet is reachable by heading.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabReaches the sheet after the page content: Cancel, then the actions in reading order (Just this day, or Request these days, Tentative, Confirm, Clear these days).
Enter / SpaceActivates the focused action.
EscFrom anywhere on the page: clears the selection, closes the sheet and shows "Selection cleared".
Week tabs and cellsStay operable while the sheet is open; they are not behind a modal.

Focus

Focus is not moved into the sheet when it appears: it stays on the cell just pressed, so the next press can be the last day. When the sheet closes (action, Cancel or Esc) focus returns to the last cell pressed, never to the document body. Buttons use the shared ring.

Labelling

The region is "Selected days". Its text reads in order: "Ben Okafor, Mon 23 – Thu 26 Nov, 4 working days, Ana, Cleo, Elif and Finn are also out 3 of these days." Buttons are named by their text; swatches are aria-hidden. The icon button is "Cancel". The overage is words, not colour alone. For the build, a polite live region should announce the state change ("From Mon 23 Nov. Now tap the last day.", then the range), because focus does not move to the sheet (see drift).

Motion

The sheet slides up 16px and fades in with the shared slide-up keyframes over --duration-base and --ease-enter. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce it only fades (fade-in, --duration-fast, linear); nothing moves. It leaves without animation.

Touch

Actions are standard 40px buttons with 8px gaps, full-width or half-width, at the bottom of the screen where a thumb reaches; the Cancel icon is a 32px square. Nothing relies on hover or a gesture.

Content

  • Member: the full name ("Ben Okafor"), as in the selection bar.
  • Picking: "From Mon 23 Nov" and "Now tap the last day. You can change week first."; the button is "Just this day".
  • Range: "Mon 23 – Thu 26 Nov" (spaced en dash between full dates; "Mon 30 Nov – Fri 4 Dec" across months of the week), then "4 working days" ("1 working day").
  • Overage: "2 days over allowance", after a middle dot.
  • Clash: members out on the same number of days are named together, joined by commas and "and": "Ben is also out 3 of these days.", "Ana, Cleo, Elif and Finn are also out 3 of these days.", "Ben is also out all of these days."
  • Actions, in order: "Request these days", "Tentative", "Confirm" (manager only), "Clear these days". Do not rename them ("Book", "Approve").
  • Hint: "Tap a day in your row to mark time off." (member) or "Tap a day in someone’s row to mark time off." (manager), then "Tap a date to see who is out."

Drift found during extraction:

  • No mock shows a member's range, an overage, a busy action or a range across months; those specimens are composed from the CSS and AC8–AC10. Fixed: a member's sheet carries data-member on its actions, so Tentative takes a full row instead of half of one.
  • Fixed: .phone-week[data-sheet] reserves 20rem while the sheet is up, so the last row can scroll clear of a range sheet with a clash line.
  • The region is position: fixed; this page overrides it inline with position: relative so specimens sit in the flow.
  • The mocks show no live-region announcement and no env(safe-area-inset-bottom) padding; both are recommended for the Angular build.
