Team switcher

The team switcher is the menu button in the top bar that names the active team and switches a person on several teams to another one (L2-054 AC11, L2-056).

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.1
  • Overview
  • Anatomy
  • States
  • Responsive behaviour
  • Accessibility
  • Content
  • Code
  • Sources

Overview

Show the switcher to anyone who belongs to two or more teams, in any role, at the end of the top bar before the account menu. It is a menu whose team items are menuitemradio: exactly one is checked, the active team. It never sets the default team; that is "Make default" in Settings → Your teams, which uses the .team-list rows (an Active and a Default badge with an icon, never colour alone).

  • Your teams
  • Add another team
  • Manage your teams

Anatomy

  1. .team-switch: relative wrapper that right-aligns the panel.
  2. .team-switch__btn: the trigger, an outlined button with .team-switch__name (ellipsis) and a chevron.
  3. .menu__label: "Your teams", decorative (the menu's accessible name says it).
  4. Team items: .menu__item[role=menuitemradio] with .menu__check (shown when aria-checked="true") and .menu__meta "Default".
  5. Divider, then "Add another team" and "Manage your teams" links, only when the person manages at least one of the teams (member state has neither).

States

Closed, open (aria-expanded="true"), hover and focus as the menu, and busy while a switch runs: aria-busy="true" on the button, a spinner in place of the chevron and "Switching…" for assistive technology. A failed switch shows the error toast and the button returns to closed.

Responsive behaviour

The button is at most 11rem wide below 40rem and 16rem above; a long team name ends in an ellipsis so the top bar never scrolls sideways at 390 px. The panel is at least 16rem wide and aligned to the button's end.

Accessibility

  • The button's accessible name is "Switch team, current team {team}"; it has aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.
  • Enter, Space or ArrowDown opens the menu on the first item; arrow keys, Home and End move; Escape closes and returns focus to the button (CDK Menu).
  • The active team is carried by aria-checked, a check icon and weight, never by colour; "Default" is text.
  • Every item is at least 32px high and opens and chooses by touch.

Content

Team names as the manager typed them. Item copy: "Add another team", "Manage your teams". Toasts: "Switched to {team}" and, on failure, "We couldn't switch teams".

Code

Angular: <gl-team-switcher [teams]="teams" [busy]="busy" (chosen)="switchTo($event)" /> in frontend/projects/components/src/lib/team-switcher, placed in the gl-top-bar end slot.
