Component · Containers and overlays

Card

A card is a bordered, softly raised surface that groups related content, such as the sign-in form or the team sheet.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

.card is the container for a self-contained group on the canvas: the sign-in and invite forms (.card.stack inside .auth-card), a settings block, an empty team. It has a 1px border, a large radius, shadow 1 and 24px padding. Two quieter relatives carry the sheet: .card-plain (border and radius only, no shadow or padding) holds tables and lists, and .grid-card is the same with horizontal scrolling for the team grid.

Do not nest cards or put a card around the whole page. A single block of text needs no card; use a divider or whitespace. Use a dialog or drawer for content that interrupts, not a card.

Anatomy

  1. Title.card__title, h4 scale; use a real heading level for the page.
  2. Header action or badgeOptional, right-aligned in .card__header.
  3. ContentFree content; stack with .stack.
  4. ContainerSurface --color-bg-surface, 1px border --color-border-default, --radius-lg, --shadow-1, 24px padding.

Variants

Choose by how much structure the content has.

Sizes

Not applicable: a card takes the width of its container; the only size choices are padding (default or <code>--flush</code>) and the content's own.

States

Only interactive cards have hover; selection is a real attribute; focus belongs to the control inside, not the card.

  • Disabled and loading cards are not used. A card whose data loads shows skeleton blocks inside; one whose data fails shows an inline message.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards are fluid: width 100% of their container; padding stays 24px at every width (the page gutter, not the card, shrinks on phones).
  • The auth card is capped at 26rem (.auth-card), or 36rem with --wide, and centred.
  • Card headers use justify-content: space-between; a long title wraps rather than pushing the action off the card.
  • In a .grid--auto a card is at least 18rem wide and never narrower than the container (min(100%, 18rem)).

Theming

The card reads --color-bg-surface, --color-border-default and --shadow-1. In dark the surface rises by lightness and the shadow is nearly invisible; the border carries the edge.

[data-theme="partner"] .card { box-shadow: none; border-color: var(--color-border-strong); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A card is a static container, usually an <article> or <section> labelled by its heading. An interactive card is not a widget: it contains one real link or button, and the pointer target may be enlarged but the single control keeps the focus and the name. No ARIA role is added to the card itself.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the card's inner link or button, not to the card.
EnterActivates that inner control.

Focus

The inner link or button shows the shared ring. The card does not show a ring because it is not focusable. Do not set tabindex on a card.

Labelling

Label the region with its heading via aria-labelledby when it is a <section> that has a heading. An interactive card's link text is the card's name ("Ben Okafor", not "Read more"). A selected card also says so in text ("Selected"), not only with the border.

Contrast

Text on surface
Muted text on surface
Edge on canvas (non-text)
Selected border on surface

Motion

Interactive cards lift 1px and deepen the shadow over --duration-fast; under reduced motion the transition collapses.

Touch

An interactive card should be at least 44px tall; the hover lift is not a signifier on touch, so the link inside needs its own affordance (underline or chevron).

Content

  • Headings are noun phrases ("Design team"), never questions.
  • One idea per card; two actions at most in a footer.
  • Footer actions order: cancel or secondary first, primary last.
  • Do not repeat the page title as the only card title.
