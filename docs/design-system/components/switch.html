Component · Inputs

Switch

A switch turns one setting on or off and applies the change immediately. Greenlit uses it for the manager's notification preferences.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

Use a switch for a setting whose effect is immediate and ongoing: "New requests" and "Decisions" e-mail notifications. The label says what is being turned on, and the state (on or off) is available as text to assistive technology.

If the change only applies after pressing Save, or is part of a set of options, use a checkbox. For one of several states use a radio group or a segmented control. Do not use a switch for an action (use a button).

Anatomy

  1. Track.switch input: 2.75rem × 1.5rem pill, --color-border-strong when off, --color-accent when on.
  2. ThumbA 1.25rem circle (::before) that slides 1.25rem on change.
  3. LabelThe setting, as a short noun phrase, in the label font.
  4. DescriptionA muted line saying what the setting does, linked with aria-describedby.

Variants

One look: a plain switch, or a switch with a description.

Sizes

Not applicable: One size: a 2.75rem × 1.5rem track in a row at least 24px high.

States

On is the real checked property of the checkbox with role="switch". Focus is forced with data-state; switches have no hover style, no invalid state and no loading state of their own (see drift).

Switch

Both themes

  • If the change is saved by a request, keep the switch where the user left it and show the result in a toast; on failure flip the switch back and say why.
  • The on/off state is also carried by the thumb position, not just the track colour.

Responsive behaviour

  • Track on the left, text on the right; the text wraps and the track stays top-aligned in descriptive rows.
  • Switch rows are at least 24px high, with stack gaps of 16px between them: at 360px each row is a full-width tap target (the whole label toggles it).
  • Do not move the switch to the right edge on desktop: a far-away control is hard to associate with its label.

Theming

The track is --color-border-strong off and --color-accent on; the thumb is --color-bg-surface with --shadow-1. In dark mode the on-track is near-white and the thumb stays the dark surface colour, so the thumb remains distinct from the track.

/* Brand-coloured switches in settings */
.settings .switch input:checked { background: var(--color-brand-strong); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <input type="checkbox" role="switch">, following the APG Switch pattern. The checked property maps to aria-checked automatically.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the switch.
SpaceToggles the switch. Enter may also toggle it in some browsers; do not rely on it.

Focus

The shared focus ring is drawn around the whole track (it is the input). The label is not separately outlined.

Labelling

The wrapping <label> names the switch; the description is linked with aria-describedby. Screen readers announce "switch, on/off"; the label should therefore not contain the words "on" or "off" or "enable" ("New requests", not "Enable new requests").

Motion

Track colour and thumb translation animate over --duration-base with --ease-standard; both become instant under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

The 44px-wide track and 24px height inside a full-width label row. The whole row toggles, so the tap target is much larger than the track.

Content

  • Label is the thing being controlled, a short noun phrase: "New requests", "Decisions". Never "Enable…" or "Turn on…".
  • The description says what happens when it is on, with the actual recipient ("Email Maya Chen when someone requests time off").
  • Use sentence case and no trailing full stop on labels.
