Chip

A compact pill that filters or toggles: pick the working days, narrow the list to who is out, remove a selected item.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

A chip is a small pressable pill. Greenlit uses it in two ways: as a toggle (aria-pressed) such as the seven working-day chips in the Work week settings and the Edit working days dialog, and as a filter that narrows a list. A chip can also be removable, with a trailing close button.

A choice of exactly one among two to four options is a segmented control; one among many is a radio group or select; an on/off setting with its own label is a switch or checkbox. Status labels that cannot be pressed are badges. A chip never submits or navigates: that is a button or link.

Anatomy

  1. LabelLabel type, one to three words. It is the accessible name.
  2. Remove button (optional).remove: a 20px circle with a close icon; aria-label names what is removed.
  3. ContainerPill, 1px strong border, surface fill; 32px tall (--control-height-sm); selected fills with the accent tint.

Variants

Filter chips

Press to filter; aria-pressed carries the on/off state. The selected chip changes fill, border and text colour, and the on/off state is also exposed in the accessibility tree.
HTML
<button class="chip" type="button" aria-pressed="true">All members</button><button class="chip" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Out this week</button><button class="chip" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Requested</button>

Day chips

.chip--day: a 48px-wide, medium-radius chip used for the working-days group. Group them in a role="group" with a label.
HTML
<button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Mon</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Tue</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Wed</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Thu</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Fri</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Sat</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Sun</button>

With icon

A leading icon is decorative (aria-hidden) and never replaces the label.
HTML
<button class="chip" type="button" aria-pressed="false"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true" viewBox="0 0 24 24"><circle cx="12" cy="8" r="4"/><path d="M4 21a8 8 0 0 1 16 0"/></svg>Dev Patel</button>

Sizes

Chips have one height, --control-height-sm (32px), which keeps them in line with small buttons. Day chips are 48px wide and 32px tall.

Standard chip sizes to its label with 12px horizontal padding; .chip--day sets min-width: 3rem so seven weekday chips align in a row.
HTML
<button class="chip" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Out this week</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Mon</button>

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state; pages get them from :hover and :focus-visible. Selection is the real aria-pressed / aria-selected attribute (or data-state="selected" for static pages).

Selected and disabled

Selected chips use --color-accent-subtle, --color-accent border and --color-fg-accent text. A disabled chip keeps the same look because the kit has no disabled style (see drift); explain why in text next to it.
HTML
<button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Mon</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Tue</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Wed</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Thu</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Fri</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Sat</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Sun</button><button class="chip chip--day" type="button" aria-pressed="true" disabled>Fri</button>

Both themes

  • Active (pressed down) has no style of its own; the selected fill on release is the feedback.
  • Loading does not apply: a chip changes immediately and applies its filter, or it is a button.

Responsive behaviour

  • Chips sit in a .cluster (flex-wrap with 8px gaps), so a row of filters wraps onto a second line rather than scrolling.
  • Seven day chips need 7 × 48px plus gaps (about 400px). At 360px they wrap to two rows; keep them in a group whose wrapping order stays Mon to Sun.
  • A long label never truncates inside a chip; shorten it. Chips never exceed their container width.
  • The chip is 32px tall at every width. In a touch-first context, keep at least 8px between chips so each target has clear space (WCAG 2.5.8 spacing).

Theming

A chip reads --color-border-strong, --color-bg-surface and --color-fg-default at rest, --color-bg-subtle on hover, and the accent trio (--color-accent-subtle, --color-accent, --color-fg-accent) when selected. In dark theme the accent tint is a deep green with light text, so the pressed state keeps 4.5:1.

/* A chip that shows a sheet status colour when selected */
.chip[data-status][aria-pressed="true"] { background: var(--status-bg); color: var(--color-on-status); border-color: var(--status-bg); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A toggle <button type="button"> with aria-pressed, per the APG button pattern (toggle button). A set of related chips lives in a role="group" with aria-label. For a single-choice filter that behaves like tabs or a listbox, use aria-selected on the proper widget role instead.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves between chips (each is a tab stop).
Space, EnterToggles the pressed state.
Space/Enter on a remove buttonRemoves the chip; move focus to the next chip, or the group, never to the page top.

Focus

The shared ring on :focus-visible. The chip radius is full, so the ring follows the pill. On a removable chip, the focus lands on the close button, not on the label.

Labelling

The visible label is the name; aria-pressed adds "toggle button, pressed". Do not change the label when pressed ("Show out" to "Hide out"), because the state is exposed already. A remove button names the thing removed: "Remove filter: Out this week". The selected state is also visible: fill, border and text colour all change, not a colour alone, and the day chips print the weekday.

Motion

Background transitions over --duration-fast; reduced-motion removes it. Removing a chip does not animate.

Touch

32px high with 8px gaps clears WCAG 2.5.8 (24px). The remove button inside is 20px with a larger hit area from the chip padding; prefer a visible "Clear filters" button on touch-first screens.

Content

  • Labels are nouns or short noun phrases in sentence case: "Out this week", "Requested".
  • Day chips use three-letter weekdays: Mon, Tue, Wed. Do not mix with single letters.
  • A group of chips needs a visible or hidden group label ("Working days").
  • Do not put counts in the chip unless the filter list shows the same count: "Requested · 3".
  • Removable chips are for a handful of items. For many, use a list with a remove action.
