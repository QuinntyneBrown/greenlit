Component · Sheet and brand

Selection bar

The floating toolbar that appears when days are selected on the sheet: it names the selection and offers Confirm, Request, Tentative and Clear.

Overview

The selection bar (.selection-bar) is the sheet's action bar. When one or more cells are selected (aria-pressed="true"), it floats over the bottom of the page and offers the four things you can do to those days: Confirm (manager only), Request, mark Tentative or Clear, plus a close button. It shows who and which dates ("Ana Ruiz · 14–16 Jul") and how many working days are included, and warns when the selection takes the person over their allowance.

It is the touch route to painting: tap a cell or drag across several, then tap an action. Use it only for acting on a selection from the sheet. For generic confirmations use a dialog; for results use a toast. The buttons are inverse buttons because the bar is on the inverse surface.

3 days working
In the app the bar sits in a fixed .selection-bar-region centred at the bottom of the viewport. Here the region is pinned inside a relatively positioned box (position: absolute) so it does not cover this page.

Anatomy

  1. Title.selection-bar__title: person and date range ("Ana Ruiz · 14–16 Jul"), label weight, never wrapped.
  2. Meta and warning.selection-bar__meta: the working-day count, plus .selection-bar__warn (alert-triangle icon, text in --color-warning-on-inverse) when over allowance.
  3. Action buttons.btn.btn--inverse.btn--sm: each starts with a swatch showing the status it paints. Labels are the verbs Confirm, Request, Tentative, Clear.
  4. Close.btn--icon with aria-label="Clear selection"; the same as Esc.

Sizes

Not applicable: the bar has one size: buttons are small (32px) and the bar grows to fit its content, wrapping when narrow.

States

The bar appears when a selection exists and disappears when it is cleared. Button states are forced here with data-state.

Busy: the pressed action shows aria-busy="true" (label hidden, clicks ignored) while the days are saved; the other buttons stay. The spinner is specified but currently not drawn on inverse buttons (see drift).
  • Hidden: no selection means no bar. It mounts with the slide-up animation (--duration-base, --ease-enter) and unmounts without one.
  • Disabled: not used. An action that does not apply is removed (no Confirm for a member). While saving, only the pressed action shows busy.
  • Print: @media print hides the whole region.

The bar is on --color-bg-inverse, which is dark in light mode and light in dark mode. Text, the translucent button fills and the warning colour all follow the inverse tokens.

Responsive behaviour

  • The region is position: fixed; bottom: --space-6, centred, with --space-4 side padding; the bar is max-width: 100% and flex-wrap: wrap.
  • At 360px the title and meta take the first row and the action buttons wrap to a second (and the close button after them); the bar grows upward. At 768px and above it is a single row.
  • The bar must not hide the cell being painted: when it is open on a phone, scroll padding at the bottom of the page (--space-6 plus its height) keeps the last member row reachable.
  • Touch: small buttons are 32px tall with --space-2 gaps (above the 24px minimum, below 44px comfortable). They are separated enough to avoid mis-taps; the close icon is 32px square.
  • The bar is hidden in print.

Theming

The bar reads --color-bg-inverse and --color-fg-inverse for its surface and text, --color-warning-on-inverse for the warning, --shadow-4 for elevation and --z-dropdown for its layer. Inverse buttons use color-mix(currentColor 12%) fills, so they stay legible whichever way the inverse surface goes.

/* Move the bar to the top on a kiosk display */
.kiosk .selection-bar-region { top: var(--space-6); bottom: auto; }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="toolbar" with aria-label="Selected days". It is a group of related buttons; on selection it mounts after the sheet and a polite live region announces "3 days selected, 2 over allowance". Focus is not moved into the bar when it appears (that would break painting by keyboard); it is reachable with Tab from the grid.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves between the bar's buttons in reading order (Confirm, Request, Tentative, Clear, Clear selection).
← →Optionally moves between toolbar buttons (APG toolbar); Tab also works.
Enter / SpaceApplies that status to every selected day.
EscFrom anywhere: clears the selection and closes the bar; focus returns to the last active grid cell.

Focus

Buttons use the shared ring, recoloured to --color-fg-inverse (.selection-bar :focus-visible) so it reads on the inverse surface. After a button is activated, focus stays on the button while saving; when the bar closes it returns to the last active cell.

Labelling

The toolbar is "Selected days"; the buttons' names are their text ("Confirm", "Request", "Tentative", "Clear"), and the close button is "Clear selection". Swatches are aria-hidden. The title and meta are plain text read in order: "Ana Ruiz · 14–16 Jul, 3 days working, 2 days over allowance". The warning has an icon and words, so it is never colour alone.

Motion

The bar slides up 16px and fades in over --duration-base (slide-up). With prefers-reduced-motion the animation is removed (the motion tokens collapse) and it simply appears.

Touch

Buttons are 32px tall with 8px gaps, the close button is a 32px square; all above the 24px target minimum. The bar is anchored to the bottom where thumbs reach. Its size must not obscure the selected row, and it does not rely on hover.

Content

  • Title: " · " with an en dash for ranges ("14–16 Jul"). Counts use plain words: "3 days working".
  • Action labels are one-word status verbs, in this order: Confirm, Request, Tentative, Clear. Do not rename them ("Approve", "Book", "Maybe").
  • Warning: " days over allowance". State the number, not the percent.
  • The close button says "Clear selection" because that is what it does.
  • A member sees Request, Tentative and Clear; only the manager sees Confirm.

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • Bug: .btn--inverse sets --btn-fg: inherit, so the busy spinner (border: 2px solid var(--btn-fg) while color is transparent) resolves to transparent: a busy inverse button shows nothing. Suggested: .btn--inverse[aria-busy="true"]::after { border-color: var(--color-fg-inverse); border-right-color: transparent; }.
  • The bar's region is position: fixed with no documentation-friendly modifier; on this page the specimen overrides it inline with position: absolute inside a positioned box.
  • Nothing in components.css reserves space so the bar does not cover the last grid row on short phones; the page needs bottom padding while the bar is open.
  • The mocks show the bar but not the live-region announcement or the toolbar arrow-key navigation; both are specified here for the Angular build.
