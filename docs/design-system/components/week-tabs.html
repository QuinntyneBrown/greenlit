Week tabs

The row of week tabs above the week strip on a phone: one tab per Monday-to-Sunday week of the month, named by its days in the month, with a marker on a week that is short of people.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newSince v1.1 (L2-063)

Overview

Week tabs (.week-tabs, Angular gl-week-tabs) choose which week of the month the week strip shows on a phone. Narrower than 48rem the month grid does not fit, so the calendar shows one Monday-to-Sunday week at a time and these tabs move between the weeks. Each tab is named by the week's days in the month ("1", "2–8", …, "30"); the first and last weeks are clipped to the month, so November 2026 has six tabs. A week that holds a short day carries a dot named "Short of people this week".

They are a compact, equal-width variant of tabs with the same tablist semantics and keyboard. Use them only to switch the week of the phone calendar. Month changes stay with period navigation in the title row; there are no previous- and next-week arrows, because the tabs already move between weeks. Do not use week tabs on tablets and desktops: from 48rem up the team grid shows the whole month.

November 2026 with the week of 2–8 Nov selected (the week that holds today). The tab list is labelled "Week" in the app; specimens on this page add a suffix so each list on the page has its own name.

Anatomy

  1. Track.week-tabs: a grid of var(--weeks) equal columns (5 by default, set inline to the month's week count), --space-1 gap and padding, on --color-bg-subtle with --radius-md.
  2. Selected tab.week-tab[aria-selected="true"]: raised on --color-bg-surface with --shadow-1, text in --color-fg-default.
  3. Short-week marker.week-tab__mark: a 6px --color-fg-default dot in the top-right corner, role="img" named "Short of people this week", so it joins the tab's name.
  4. Tab.week-tab: caption type, medium weight, tabular figures, never wrapped, --color-fg-muted until hovered or selected.

Variants

One style; what varies is how many weeks the month has, which week is selected and which weeks carry the marker.

Short week selected: the week of 23–29 Nov holds three short days, so its tab carries the marker. Its accessible name is "23–29 Short of people this week".
Short week not selected: the marker stays on its tab whichever week is shown, so the person can see where the short days are before going there.
Five weeks: a month that starts on a Monday (March 2027) has five tabs; --weeks: 5 is the default, so the inline value can be left off. A month can have four to six weeks.

Sizes

Not applicable: week tabs have one size: each tab is at least 24px tall (the target minimum) and the columns share the full width of the phone.

States

Focus is forced with the shared data-state="focus". Hover has no documentation hook (see drift), so it is described rather than drawn. Selection is the real aria-selected attribute.

PartDefaultFocusSelectedSelected + focusWith marker
Week tab
  • Hover: an unselected tab's text darkens from --color-fg-muted to --color-fg-default. Phones rarely hover; the state exists for narrow desktop windows.
  • Disabled: not used. Every week of the month can be shown, offline included: the week tabs keep working while no cell takes a tap.
  • Changing month rebuilds the tabs and selects the week that holds today in the current month, otherwise the first week.
  • Pressing a short-days run selects the tab of the run's week and opens its first day in the week strip.

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

  • Week tabs show only below 48rem (768px), the width at which the month grid no longer fits; from 48rem up the page shows the team grid and no week tabs.
  • The track is full width and its columns are equal (minmax(0, 1fr)), so nothing scrolls sideways. At 320px six tabs are about 45px wide each, enough for the widest name ("23–29") in caption type; names never wrap.
  • The track sits between the "Days short of people" card and the week strip, --space-3 from each (.phone-week > * + *).
  • If a future layout made the names wider than their column, shorten the name rather than letting it wrap or scroll.

Theming

The track reads --color-bg-subtle, the selected tab --color-bg-surface and --shadow-1, text --color-fg-muted and --color-fg-default, and the marker --color-fg-default. All are role tokens, so the dark theme needs no extra rule: the track darkens, the selected tab is the raised surface and the marker turns light.

/* A brand-coloured marker for a product that wants the short week to stand out more */
.week-tab__mark { background: var(--color-danger-solid); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA APG Tabs pattern with automatic activation, exactly like gl-tabs: role="tablist" labelled "Week" holds role="tab" buttons, each aria-controls the week strip, which is the role="tabpanel" labelled "Week of 2–8 Nov". Roving tabindex: only the selected tab is in the tab order (tabindex="-1" on the others).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves focus into the selected tab, then on to the week strip's first date.
Arrow Left / Arrow RightMoves focus to the previous or next week, wrapping at the ends, and shows it (automatic activation).
Home / EndMoves to and shows the first or last week of the month.
Enter / SpaceShows the focused week (already shown with automatic activation; kept for L2-063 AC15).
EscNot handled by the tabs; it reaches the page and clears a selection in the mark sheet.

Focus

The shared ring (--color-focus-ring, 2px offset) is drawn around the tab. The track's --space-1 padding keeps the ring inside the track's rounded edge. Changing week keeps focus on the tab; it is not moved into the strip.

Labelling

The tablist is "Week". Each tab is named by its days ("2–8"); a tab with the marker is "23–29 Short of people this week", because the marker is a named image inside the button. The marker is never the only carrier: the "Days short of people" card lists the same runs in words. The panel's name ("Week of 23–29 Nov") repeats the month, which the tab names leave out.

Motion

None. The selected surface moves instantly; there is no sliding indicator, so reduced motion changes nothing.

Touch

Each tab is at least 24px tall (--target-min, the WCAG 2.2 AA 2.5.8 minimum) and about 45–55px wide on a phone, with 4px between tabs. That is smaller than the 44px comfortable target of tabs; see drift.

Content

  • Name a tab by its days in the month: the first and last day joined by an en dash ("2–8", "23–29"), or a single number when the week holds one day of the month ("1", "30"). No month, weekday or "Week 2".
  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday and never cross into another month.
  • The marker's name is always "Short of people this week".
  • The list's name is "Week"; the panel it controls is "Week of 2–8 Nov".

Drift found during extraction (documented, not changed in components.css):

  • .week-tab:hover has no data-state="hover" twin, so the hover state cannot be forced on this page. Suggested: .week-tab[data-state="hover"] beside the :hover rule.
  • Tabs are --target-min (24px) tall: they pass 2.5.8 but are smaller than the 44px of .tab. Consider min-height: var(--target-comfortable) if testing shows mis-taps.
  • The mocks show only six-week months; the five-week specimen is composed from the CSS (--weeks defaults to 5).
