Progress bar

A progress bar shows how far something has got: a determinate or indeterminate track, plus the segmented usage bar that shows a member's allowance.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

Progress bar (.progress) reports a task that takes time and has a known or unknown length. Usage bar (.usage-bar) is a different job with the same look: a read-only measure of allowance, split by day status (confirmed, requested), as seen in the year overview and the member list.

Use a determinate bar when you can say how much is done (importing a holiday list, uploading). Use an indeterminate bar when you cannot. For a short wait on one control use a spinner or a busy button; for content that is loading use a skeleton. The usage bar is not interactive and never a substitute for the numbers beside it.

Anatomy

  1. Bar.progress__bar fills the track from the left to --value and animates its width over --duration-slow.
  2. Track.progress: full-width 0.5rem pill on --color-bg-subtle-hover with role="progressbar" and an accessible name.

Variants

Determinate

The bar is set by the custom property --value on the bar and mirrored in aria-valuenow. Success turns it green on completion.
HTML
<div style="display: flex; flex-direction: column; gap: var(--space-3); width: 100%;"><div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Importing 2027 bank holidays" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" aria-valuenow="25"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value: 25%"></div></div><div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Importing 2027 bank holidays" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" aria-valuenow="64"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value: 64%"></div></div><div class="progress progress--success" role="progressbar" aria-label="Importing 2027 bank holidays" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" aria-valuenow="100"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value: 100%"></div></div></div>

Tones

Default (accent), .progress--success and .progress--danger. The tone never carries state alone: pair it with text such as "Upload failed".
HTML
<div style="display: flex; flex-direction: column; gap: var(--space-3); width: 100%;"><div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Upload progress" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" aria-valuenow="70"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value: 70%"></div></div><div class="progress progress--success" role="progressbar" aria-label="Upload complete" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" aria-valuenow="100"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value: 100%"></div></div><div class="progress progress--danger" role="progressbar" aria-label="Upload failed" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100" aria-valuenow="40"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value: 40%"></div></div></div>

Indeterminate

.progress--indeterminate slides a 40% bar across the track. Omit aria-valuenow so assistive technology reports it as busy rather than 0%.
HTML
<div class="progress progress--indeterminate" role="progressbar" aria-label="Checking your connection" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100"><div class="progress__bar"></div></div>

Usage bar

Segments are coloured by data-status (confirmed green, requested yellow) and sized with --value as a share of the allowance. The whole bar is one role="img" with a full-sentence label.
HTML
<div style="display: flex; flex-direction: column; gap: var(--space-3); width: 100%;"><div class="usage-bar" role="img" aria-label="20 of 25 days taken or booked, 0 requested"><span class="usage-bar__seg" data-status="confirmed" style="--value: 80%"></span><span class="usage-bar__seg" data-status="requested" style="--value: 0%"></span></div><div class="usage-bar" role="img" aria-label="12 of 25 days taken or booked, 5 requested"><span class="usage-bar__seg" data-status="confirmed" style="--value: 48%"></span><span class="usage-bar__seg" data-status="requested" style="--value: 20%"></span></div><div class="usage-bar" role="img" aria-label="0 of 25 days taken or booked, 0 requested"><span class="usage-bar__seg" data-status="confirmed" style="--value: 0%"></span><span class="usage-bar__seg" data-status="requested" style="--value: 0%"></span></div></div>

Sizes

The track is 0.5rem (8px) tall at all times and fills its container. There is no size modifier: change the container's width, not the bar's height. Where a taller bar would be needed (a hero upload), the bar is still 8px and the text above it carries the status; a thicker bar adds no information.

States

A progress bar moves from empty to full; the usage bar is a static snapshot.

ElementEmptyIn progressCompleteIndeterminateFailed
Progress

Both themes

  • Indeterminate loops a 1.4s slide. Under prefers-reduced-motion the animation stops and the bar becomes a static 60% opaque full-width fill.
  • Complete should switch to the success tone and announce "complete" in a live region; hide the bar after a short delay if it adds nothing.
  • Failed keeps the bar where it stopped, turns it danger and shows the reason and a retry beside it.
  • The determinate fill animates its width (--duration-slow); skipped by reduced motion because transition durations collapse.

Responsive behaviour

  • The bar is width: 100% of its container at every viewport; there is no breakpoint behaviour.
  • Put the label and percentage above the bar in a row that wraps (label first) so they are not squeezed beside it at 360px.
  • In the year grid the usage bar sits in a table cell with the numbers; at narrow widths the table scrolls, the bar keeps its aspect.
  • Never rely on the bar for a number: print the figure ("20 of 25 days") next to it; in print the track still shows but the colours may not.

Theming

The track is --color-bg-subtle-hover and the fill --color-accent; success and danger use --color-success-solid and --color-danger-solid. Usage segments take the sheet status colours (--color-status-confirmed, --color-status-requested) through --status-bg, so the bar always matches the calendar. All are retuned for dark mode so the fill keeps 3:1 against the track.

.progress--brand .progress__bar { background: var(--color-brand); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The WAI-ARIA progressbar role: role="progressbar" with aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax and, for determinate bars, aria-valuenow. An indeterminate bar omits aria-valuenow. The usage bar is a static graphic, so it is role="img" with a sentence label, not a progressbar. A progressbar is not focusable.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the bar: it is not interactive. Place any cancel button after it in the tab order.
EscNot handled by the bar; cancel is a separate button.

Focus

Progress bars are not focusable and take no focus ring. If a long task has a Cancel button, that button follows the shared focus rules (button).

Labelling

Name the bar for the task ("Importing 2027 bank holidays") with aria-label or aria-labelledby pointing at visible text. Update aria-valuenow at a sensible cadence (every 10%, not every frame). For the usage bar write the figures: "20 of 25 days taken or booked, 0 requested"; the colours add nothing for a screen reader.

Contrast

Accent fill on track
Success fill on track
Danger fill on track
Confirmed segment on track
Label text on surface

Motion

Determinate fill grows over --duration-slow; indeterminate slides continuously for 1.4s per cycle. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the indeterminate bar stops and shows a static 60% opaque full-width fill, and the width transition is shortened to 0.01ms. Do not substitute a pulsing animation.

Touch

The bar is display only and has no touch target. If the task can be cancelled, give the Cancel button at least 40px height, with 8px of space between it and the bar.

Content

  • Write the label above the bar as a verb phrase in the progressive: "Importing 2027 bank holidays".
  • Show the number next to a determinate bar ("64%", or "3 of 5 holidays") so the bar is a visual aid, not the data.
  • On completion say what is now true: "2027 bank holidays imported". On failure: what stopped and what is safe, plus Retry.
  • Usage bar copy states both parts of the allowance: "20 of 25 days taken or booked, 0 requested". Use the product's words (taken, booked, requested), not "used".
  • Do not show an indeterminate bar for more than a moment without an explanation of what is happening.
