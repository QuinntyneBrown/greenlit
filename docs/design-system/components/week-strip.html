Component · Sheet and brand

Week strip

One week of the team on a phone: the seven dates as buttons, a row of seven cells per member with their days left, the Out count under each working day, and bands on short days.

Overview

The week strip (.week-strip, Angular gl-week-strip) is the phone form of the team grid. Narrower than 48rem a month of day columns does not fit, so the calendar shows one Monday-to-Sunday week of the team at full width. The strip is the role="tabpanel" of the week tabs and is labelled with its week ("Week of 2–8 Nov").

It has four layers: a row of seven dates that open the day panel ("who is out?"); one row per member, in the grid's order, with their name, "You" on the person's own row and their days left; seven sheet cells per row, which are buttons on rows the person may change and images on the others; and the Out line, the grid's coverage counts reused under each working day. Short days are banded down their column. Marking time off is two taps on one row, a first and a last day, finished in the mark sheet; there is no painting or dragging on a phone.

Anatomy

  1. Dates.week-strip__days: seven .week-strip__date buttons, each an initial (__initial: M T W Th F S Su, aria-hidden) over a 32px round number (__num). Weekend and holiday dates are data-off (red).
  2. Band.week-strip__bands: an aria-hidden layer of seven .week-strip__band columns behind the rows; data-short tints a short day, data-open outlines the open date.
  3. Member heading.week-strip__who: 24px avatar, __name (ellipsis), __you ("You", on the person's own row) and __left (days left, red with data-over).
  4. Cells.week-strip__cells > .week-strip__slot > .cell: 44px tall rounded sheet cells with the grid's colour, glyph and name. aria-pressed="true" marks the selection, data-edge its first and last day.
  5. Short-day columnThe tinted band reads through the cells of a short day so the whole column is marked, not one cell.
  6. Out line.week-strip__out: one .cover per working day under a rule, with the Out text and name of the grid ("4 out, 3 in. Below the minimum of 4"); empty under weekends and holidays.

Responsive behaviour

  • The strip replaces the team grid narrower than 48rem (768px), the grid's own breakpoint, so a narrow desktop window gets the week too. From 48rem up the page shows the grid and no strip; a tablet in portrait (820px) keeps the grid.
  • Dates, cells, bands and the Out line share one repeat(7, minmax(0, 1fr)) grid, so the columns always line up and nothing scrolls sideways. At 320px a column is about 38px wide; at 390px about 47px.
  • Names truncate with an ellipsis; "You" and the days left never wrap. On very narrow phones the name gives way first.
  • The page reserves 9rem of bottom padding (.phone-week) so the hint or the mark sheet at the foot never hides the last row.
  • Touch: every cell is 44px tall and at least 34px wide with 4px between columns; dates are about 50px tall. All are above the 24px minimum.

Theming

The card reads --color-bg-surface, --color-border-default and --radius-lg; dates read --color-fg-default, --color-fg-subtle (initials), --color-danger-fg (red days), --color-brand (today's ring) and the inverse pair for the open date. Bands are color-mix() of --color-fg-default at 7%, so they darken the light surface and lighten the dark one. Cells take the status tokens of the sheet cell.

/* A stronger band where the minimum matters most */
.week-strip__band[data-short] { background: color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-danger-solid) 10%, transparent); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The strip is the role="tabpanel" of the week tabs (APG Tabs), labelled "Week of 2–8 Nov". Inside it is plain content in reading order, not an ARIA grid: dates are buttons with aria-expanded (APG Disclosure, controlling the day panel), editable cells are toggle buttons with aria-pressed, read-only cells and Out counts are role="img" with a name, and member rows are list items. The band layer is aria-hidden.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the dates left to right, then each editable row's cells left to right. Read-only cells, blanks and the Out line are not stops.
Enter / Space on a dateOpens the day panel for that date; again closes it.
Enter / Space on a cellFirst press picks the first day; a press on the same row picks the last day; a press on another row starts again there.
EscClears a selection from anywhere on the page ("Selection cleared").
Arrow keysNot used inside the strip; arrows move between weeks on the week tabs.

Focus

Dates use the shared ring. Cells use the sheet cell's inset ring: ink (--color-on-status) on coloured cells, --color-focus-ring on working cells. Changing week moves no focus; after a mark saves, focus stays on the last cell tapped.

Labelling

Dates are named "See who is out Mon 2 Nov" (the initial and number are hidden duplicates); today's number has aria-current="date". Cells are named "Ana Ruiz, Tue 3 Nov: Working" / "Confirmed" / "Requested" / "Tentative" / "Not working" and say "selected" through aria-pressed, so colour is never the only carrier. Out counts are named "1 out, 6 in" or "4 out, 3 in. Below the minimum of 4". The band repeats what the Out count and the short-days card already say in words. Blanks are empty and silent.

Motion

Only the date number's fill changes, over --duration-fast; the motion tokens collapse under prefers-reduced-motion, so it switches instantly.

Touch

Cells are 44px tall and a seventh of the width; two taps (first and last day) replace dragging, so no gesture needs precision or a path. Rows the person may not change ignore taps entirely rather than opening an error.

Content

  • Weekday initials are M, T, W, Th, F, S, Su; the week starts on Monday.
  • Date names: "See who is out" + short weekday, day and month ("See who is out Mon 2 Nov").
  • Cell names: ", : ", with the statuses of the grid.
  • Days left: "5 left"; over the allowance a minus sign and red ("-2 left").
  • "You" marks the person's own row, nothing else.
  • The panel label: "Week of 2–8 Nov", "Week of 1 Nov", "Week of 30 Nov".

Drift found during extraction:

  • Fixed: the selected and first/last rings now use --color-on-status on status cells and --color-fg-default only on empty cells, as the sheet cell does, so the dark theme keeps ink rings on colour.
  • .week-strip__who reads var(--space-1-5, 0.375rem); there is no --space-1-5 token, so the fallback always applies. Use --space-1 or --space-2, or add the token.
  • No mock shows a week outside the month (weeks "1" and "30"); the blank specimen is composed from .week-strip__blank and AC2. The date placeholder is an empty aria-hidden element (.week-strip__date[data-blank]), never a disabled button, which would be an unnamed control.
  • No mock shows the strip offline or loading; the offline specimen applies AC14 with the read-only cell form.
