Sheet interaction

Painting days, selecting cells, requesting and confirming time off, with the same behaviour on touch, pointer and keyboard.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

The sheet is the product. A member colours their own days; the manager confirms them; everyone sees the same month. Every action has a pointer path, a touch path and a keyboard path, and every state has a word or a symbol as well as a colour. Components: team grid, sheet cell, segmented control (the paint picker), legend, selection bar, period navigation and request row.

When to use which

GoalMemberManager
Pencil in a dayPaint Tentative (blue)Same, on any row
Ask for time offPaint Requested (yellow), or select and choose RequestSame; their own requests need no approval step
Confirm time offNot available; Confirm is absent from the barSelect requested days and choose Confirm, or Approve in Requests
DeclineCannotDecline in Requests, optional reason
Take a day backPaint Clear, or select and Clear, on their own rowClear any row
Edit someone else's rowNot allowed; cells are read-onlyAllowed on any member
Mark a holidayNot allowedSettings, holidays; shown red for everyone

Two ways to change cells, chosen with the paint picker:

ModeGestureBest for
Select (default)Tap or click a cell to toggle it; Shift+click or drag to extend; then choose a verb in the selection barMixed changes, touch, careful work
Paint statusChoose Confirmed, Requested, Tentative or Clear, then click or drag across cells to apply that statusFast bulk entry, like the sheet it replaces
  • Select is the default so a stray tap never changes data.
  • The picker's status buttons are radios: exactly one is active.

Structure

The calendar page is one interaction loop. The member chooses a paint in the segmented picker, taps or drags across their own cells (or selects them), and the selection bar offers the verbs that apply. A member requests; the manager confirms. Everything below happens on the same sheet.

States

A cell has one status plus an optional selection. Each status has a colour, a symbol and a name.

StatusCellSymbolAccessible name endsWho can set
Working (no row)noneWorking-
Tentative?TentativeMember (own), manager
Requested…RequestedMember (own), manager
Confirmed✓ConfirmedManager only
Weekend / holidaynoneNot workingRule / manager (holidays)
  • Selected: an inset outline via aria-pressed="true"; the selection bar shows the member, date range and count of working days.
  • Read-only: cells that the viewer may not change render as non-interactive cells (no button), not as disabled buttons.
  • Over allowance: the bar shows an inline warning (icon and "2 days over allowance"); the New request dialog repeats it as a warning alert. Never blocked.
  • Saving and failure: the cell changes immediately; if the request fails it reverts and an error toast says so. Offline shows the connection banner and keeps the last known sheet.
  • Declined: the days return to working; the member sees the reason in the request detail.

Responsive behaviour

  • 360px: the sheet scrolls horizontally inside its card with the member column sticky at the start; the paint picker scrolls within its row. Cells are at least 24px wide so a thumb can hit one; use Select mode and the selection bar for precision. The bar spans the width above the safe area, actions wrap in two rows.
  • 768px: a month fits with light scroll; the picker sits beside the period title.
  • 1024px and up: a whole month is visible; the bar is centred and capped in width at the bottom of the viewport.
  • The pointer-fine experience adds hover tint and drag. Touch uses tap-to-toggle and long-press-and-drag to extend; the page still scrolls when the gesture starts outside the cells.

Accessibility

KeyAction
TabMoves into the sheet at the first interactive cell, then out. The grid is a single tab stop; arrows move within it.
← → ↑ ↓Move to the neighbouring day or member. Weekend and holiday cells are skipped.
Home / EndFirst / last working day of the row. Ctrl+Home / End go to the first / last cell of the sheet.
Space / EnterSelect mode: start a selection on the cell; the arrow keys then extend it along the row, and Enter again ends the extending. Paint mode: apply the active status to the cell.
PageUp / PageDownPrevious / next month; focus returns to the same member and day.
Shift+arrowsExtend the selection along the row from the anchor cell.
EscClear the selection and return focus to the cell.
Tab from the sheetNext stop is the selection bar's toolbar when anything is selected.
  • Grid semantics: role="grid" with a label naming the month, rowheader for members, columnheader with a full-date aria-label ("Tue 24 Nov"). Each cell's name is "Member, Weekday day Month: Status".
  • Toolbar: the selection bar is role="toolbar" aria-label="Selected days" with roving arrow keys; it appears after the sheet in the DOM, and appearing it announces "3 days selected" through the polite status.
  • Result: after Confirm or Request, a toast reports the change (polite). Focus stays on the last cell so the user can continue.
  • Paint picker is a radio group named "Paint"; arrow keys move among the statuses.
  • Colour is not the only carrier: symbol, accessible name and legend words. The red of weekends is also text in the name ("Not working").
  • Touch and parity: every drag has a tap equivalent (select, then verb); targets are at least 24px wide with 2px gaps, and the bar buttons are 32px high with 8px between.
  • Motion: the selection outline does not animate. Reduced motion changes nothing here.

Content

  • Verbs in the bar: Confirm, Request, Tentative, Clear (always the status names); icon-only close is "Clear selection".
  • Bar title: "Member · range" in short dates: "Ana Ruiz · 23–25 Nov". Meta: "3 days working" (weekends and holidays are not counted).
  • Over allowance: "2 days over allowance". No judgement words.
  • Results: "3 days marked confirmed for Ana", "Request sent for 23–25 Nov. Maya will see it in Requests."
  • Decline reason: optional, shown to the member as written.
  • The legend always lists Confirmed, Requested, Tentative, Weekend / holiday, in that order.
