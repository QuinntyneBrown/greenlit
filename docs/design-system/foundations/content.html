Content

Voice, casing, numbers and dates, and the formulas for errors and empty states.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Greenlit talks like a helpful colleague who has the sheet open: plain, brief, in the user's terms. It uses the domain vocabulary and never invents synonyms: Team, Member, Day, Holiday, Manager; statuses Tentative, Requested, Confirmed.

Voice and tone

  • Plain and warm, never jokey about someone's time off. Short sentences. Active voice.
  • Sentence case for every label, heading and button. Proper nouns and weekday and month names are capitalised.
  • Say what will happen: “Request days”, “Confirm 5 days”, not “OK”.
  • Don't blame the user. “That password is too short” beats “Invalid password”.

Numbers, dates and times

  • Days are counted in whole days: “5 days”, “1 day”, “0.5 days” only if half days exist (they do not yet).
  • Dates: “Tue 14 July” in context, “14 July 2026” alone, “13–17 July” for ranges in the same month, “30 Jun – 3 Jul” across months. Never US-only numeric dates.
  • Dates and times follow the team's time zone, not the viewer's.
  • Right-align and tabulate counts in tables.

Error messages

Error messages: what happened, why, what to do

Do: “Couldn’t send your request. You’re offline. Reconnect and try again.”

Don’t: “Error 500.” “Something went wrong.” “Invalid input.”

Empty states

Empty states: what this is, what to do

Do: “No requests waiting. When a member requests days, you’ll see them here.”

Don’t: “No data.”
